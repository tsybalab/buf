## 0. Central question

The Berliner Umgehungsformel asks whether formally admissible procedural devices were used to obtain a legal result without adequately establishing the facts on which that result depended.

The journalistic short form — five judges, five ways of not establishing functional capacity — is easy to attack, because the procedural situations differ. That is not the thesis.

The thesis is a **criterion**, applied to five empirical cases whose shorthand labels are historically U, A, NP, KB and TB\*.

**Central question:**

Did the courts independently decide the legally material questions before them — or did different procedural answers repeatedly exclude one and the same unresolved fact from the subject of judicial inquiry?

The answer is not assumed in advance.

A court order is not the only source of confirmation for its own correctness.

The formula did not arise because five decisions shared the same motives. It arose because different judicial answers *can* perform the same function: leaving the central question unresolved. Whether they do so is to be tested against the original filings, procedural law, and the actual content of each proceeding.

---

## 1. What this analysis does not claim

It does not claim that a decision is wrong because it differs from the others.

It does not claim that five courts answered five identical medical questions differently.

It does not claim that the documents already prove the person was incapable of acting on a given calendar day.

It does not claim that a court must appoint an expert in every case. Failure to appoint one is not already a breach of § 144 ZPO. An application under § 144 ZPO does not always require a decision of its own.

It claims no motives, no collusion, and no system of the entire German judiciary.

It claims the opposite of the usual short defence:

Not: *The court was not allowed to apply the statute.*  
But: *The court could apply a statute in each case.* Whether that statute answers the question that was actually posed is a different test.

## 2. Method

### 2.1 Centre of the inquiry

An earlier draft measured court decisions mainly against their own reasons. That repeats the mechanism BUF is meant to study: taking the court’s procedural wording as a sufficient answer without checking whether it resolves the question posed.

Therefore, before reading the judgment:

1. the *Verfahrensgegenstand* as the party opened it;
2. *Parteivorbringen*;
3. the evidence and applications on the file;
4. the court’s procedural duties (*Gehör*, *Hinweis*, *Beweiswürdigung*);
5. only then the court’s qualification and reasons.

### 2.2 Kinds of source

Every material statement carries one of these marks:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dok** | Fact from a primary piece in the BUF corpus |
| **Partei** | Submission of the person concerned |
| **Gericht** | Statement of the decision or the court’s letter |
| **Analyse** | Conclusion of this working aid |
| **Norm** | Legal rule |
| **Offen** | Not verifiable, because the full text is missing from the corpus |
| **Nutzer** | Author’s statement of 4 November 2026, not attested verbatim in the corpus |

### 2.3 Path of qualification

A court need not make an unfounded medical finding in order to leave the central question unresolved. It can:

1. re-qualify the question;
2. narrow the subject-matter;
3. treat evidence as irrelevant;
4. refuse an expert report;
5. apply a procedural bar;
6. close the proceeding without establishing the original fact.

Each step can be lawful. **Analyse:** The sequence can still be a chain of procedural error if the qualification makes proof impossible and that impossibility is then used as the ground of refusal.

What is tested is not only the last sentence in the reasons. What is tested is the path from the first application to the subject of the decision.

An application under § 144 ZPO does not always require a decision of its own. If the court has **lawfully** treated the underlying fact as legally irrelevant, an expert report may fall away. Missing a separate ruling is then not an independent error.

The test starts with the **qualification**: was irrelevance correctly determined? Did it arise only after a prior narrowing of the subject? Only if the qualification is wrong — if the original fact remained material — does the absence of an expert report need explanation.

### 2.4 Two levels

Not everything is compressed into “bypass proved / not proved”.

**Level I — the individual proceeding.** Was there a procedural violation? Which? Was it material? Could it affect the outcome? Which evidence supports or refutes that?

**Level II — comparative BUF.** Is there a recurring way of handling functional uncertainty? Is it the result of different lawful devices? Does a wrong definition of the inquiry repeat? Does non-performance of judicial duties repeat? Does shifting the burden of establishing the fact onto the applicant repeat? Can a common mechanism be named without motives and without coordination among judges?

The absence of a proven causal violation in one judgment does not automatically refute the pattern. The pattern does not automatically prove the unlawfulness of every judgment.

### 2.5 Necessity test, newly bounded

An alternative legal ground refutes the bypass hypothesis only if it independently carries the result. For each alternative:

- Is it legally self-standing?
- Does it cover all applications that remained material?
- Is it correctly applied?
- Does it depend on the same unestablished fact?
- Did it arise only after a prior narrowing of the subject-matter?
- Were procedural duties breached in applying it?
- Does it lawfully make the underlying fact irrelevant, so that § 144 ZPO needs no decision of its own?

If it survives this test, it is sufficient. If it does not, it must not automatically refute BUF.

### 2.6 TB\*

TB\* is not a fifth annex case of the original BUF. It is a **transfer test**: can the formula be applied to a different subject-matter? The result of this row must neither carry nor replace the four original cases.

### 2.7 Epistemic status shift

Heuristic alone is not yet a bypass. A bypass can arise when the **epistemic status** of a sentence shifts in the reasons: from what the file contains to what the conclusion is then used as.

For each case:

1. **Ausgangsinformation** — what the court actually knew (**Dok**, **Partei**, court record).
2. **Heuristik** — which simplified rule or everyday assumption is applied.
3. **Schlussfolgerung** — which conclusion is drawn from it.
4. **Erkenntnisstatus** — how the court presents that conclusion: *assumption*, *indicator*, or *established fact*.
5. **Entscheidungsfunktion** — what role it plays in the legal result.
6. **Kontrolle** — whether alternative explanations and the limits of the inference were tested.

The three statuses are not interchangeable. An assumption later used as fact is a status shift. An indicator that carries like final proof is a status shift. A procedural presumption that replaces a physical event is a status shift in this test only where the statute requires the physical event — not where it does not, as with *Zustellung*.

The shift is read in the wording of the reasons — not from an imputed intent.

That is not the only epistemic type.

### 2.8 Epistemic exclusion (*epistemischer Ausschluss*)

**Epistemic exclusion** — leaving submitted evidence or applications without substantive consideration. The term is kept as a working concept of this analysis.

Not every epistemic finding is a status shift. **Ausschluss** is present when an application or a document on the file receives *no* epistemic status at all: neither assumption nor indicator nor established fact, and not even marked inadmissibility. It does not appear in the reasons. That is the finding on the file, not already a duty to decide.

A shift presupposes a sentence whose status moves. Exclusion is a null status. The two types are not the same.

Type case: NP June (“nichts veranlasst”). The June applications, including § 144 ZPO, do not appear in the reply. That is the observation of a null status, not already proof that a separate decision on the expert report was due. That duty arises only if the underlying fact remained material.

## 3. Methodological audit of the previous draft

**Analyse.** The draft of 3/4 November 2026 introduced the necessity test, the four mechanism types and the counter-test. That remains usable.

It also risked three errors.

**First.** It often reconstructed the operative part from the court’s own reasons and asked whether the slogan carried the tenor. That is internally logical. It depends on the court’s qualification. Once the court has already narrowed the question, every other ground looks “independent”.

**Second.** It denied a causal bypass of the slogan in U and TB\* because other sentences carried the tenor. It did not sufficiently ask whether those sentences arose only after the subject-matter had been narrowed.

**Third.** It played heterogeneity of paths against a contradiction of medical findings. That was right against a journalistic overstatement. It must not dissolve a *real* incompatibility merely because different statutes were used.

**Correction in this version:** First the application. Then the file. Then the qualification. Then the reasons.

## 4. NP — *Erinnerung*, service, „nichts veranlasst“

**Court:** Amtsgericht Lichtenberg, enforcement court.  
**Two documents, not one.**

### 4.1 What the party first applied for

**Dok / Gericht** (order of 28 March 2025, restating the *Erinnerung* of 27 January 2025): The debtor opposes enforcement. The total sum is incorrect. The original claim of €45.31 was discharged by payment on 31 July 2023. The enforcement title was not properly served. Attachment is impossible because he receives *Bürgergeld*. Enforcement is contrary to public policy.

**Offen:** The full text of the *Erinnerung* of 27 January 2025 is not in the BUF corpus. Reconstruction rests on the court’s restatement.

**Analyse:** The original subject is not “state of health”. It is the sum, performance, **service of the title**, exemption from attachment, public policy.

### 4.2 What the file says about service

**Gericht** (28 March 2025): The *Gerichtsvollzieher* submitted that the formal enforcement requirements were met; service of the *Vollstreckungsbescheid* was in order. After inspecting the bailiff’s special file, the *Vollstreckungsbescheid* of Amtsgericht Mayen of 17 June 2024 is on the file. According to the bailiff, the debtor was registered at that address until 1 July 2024. Proper service is therefore to be assumed.

**Nutzer** (4 November 2026): The court knew that the title had remained at the window; it was not physically received because mail did not function in the building. That matters, because the court later wrote that there was nothing to confirm.

**Dok:** The documented wording of the letter of 7 June 2025 is not “nichts zu bestätigen”. It is:

> auf Ihr Schreiben vom 4.6.2025 hier nichts veranlasst werden kann.

**EN:** … no action can be taken here on your letter of 4.6.2025.

**Norm:** Proper *Zustellung* under the ZPO is not the same as physically receiving the paper. Missing receipt does not automatically refute service.

**Analyse:** The window-and-mail account is an author’s statement, not court knowledge attested verbatim in the corpus. What the corpus shows: the service objection was raised. The court treated it as settled via registration and the bailiff’s file. That the readable extract of the order does not find physical receipt says nothing, by itself, about statutory *Zustellung*.

### 4.3 What the court decided in March

**Gericht:** The *Erinnerung* is already inadmissible and in any event unfounded, § 766 ZPO. Insofar as the debtor raises substantive objections (performance under § 362 BGB), they are not to be examined in the *Erinnerung*. Examination remains for the court of the main action. The requirements of § 750 ZPO are met.

**Norm:** § 766 ZPO separates *Erinnerung* and the main action. § 750 ZPO makes service of the title a condition of enforcement. Service is therefore *not* the same category as performance.

**Analyse, Level I:** Carving out performance can be lawful. The enforcement court was not allowed to push service aside with the same formula. It did not push it aside. It assumed it as given. Whether the author’s account of the window touches the *statutory* facts of *Zustellung* cannot be finished from the corpus. Missing physical receipt is not automatic counter-proof.

### 4.4 What was on the same file in June

**Partei / Dok** (letter of 4 June 2025): *Wiedereinsetzung* under § 233 ZPO; court-ordered expert report under § 144 ZPO; the course packet; the impairment was not limited to the recorded periods; stay until the report; request to consider a coordinated report with a parallel case; plus aims as to enforcement, amount, instalments.

**Gericht / Dok** (letter of 7 June 2025, drawn up 10 June 2025):

> es wird darauf hingewiesen, dass auf Ihr Schreiben vom 4.6.2025 hier nichts veranlasst werden kann. Es ist durch Sie keine sofortige Beschwerde eingelegt worden. Mangels Beschwerde ist der Wiedereinsetzungsantrag bereits nicht nachvollziehbar.

**EN:** It is noted that no action can be taken here on your letter of 4.6.2025. You have not lodged an immediate appeal (*sofortige Beschwerde*). For lack of an appeal, the application for reinstatement (*Wiedereinsetzung*) already cannot be followed.

As a precaution, again: substantive objections are not examined in the *Erinnerung* under § 766 ZPO. For an instalment agreement he should turn to the creditor’s counsel.

There is not a word on § 144 ZPO. Not a word on the documents filed. Not a word on coordination. Not a word on the service objection.

**Analyse, path of qualification:** The court read the June applications as a continuation of the disposed *Erinnerung*: no *Beschwerde*, therefore nothing to arrange. § 144 ZPO and the new documents do not appear in the reply.

Coordination creates no duty to join two *Amtsgerichte*. It is not the load-bearing complaint. The complaint is not the missing expert ruling as such. It is whether this qualification **lawfully** made the June applications irrelevant. If it did, the expert report could fall away without a decision of its own.

**Level I, NP March:** For performance as the subject of an *Erinnerung*, § 766 ZPO can carry. For service, what remains is whether the objection was tested against the statutory concept of *Zustellung* — not whether missing receipt already refutes it. The court treated service as settled.

**Level I, NP June:** No functional finding. **Procedural exclusion** by qualification. The open legal question is whether the qualification (no *Beschwerde* → nothing to arrange) was lawful. If it was, § 144 ZPO could fall away without a decision of its own. The bypass hypothesis then falls. It stands insofar as the qualification was wrong and the underlying fact remained material.

### 4.5 Epistemic status shift

Two layers, two shifts.

**March — service**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | Objection of defective service; *Gerichtsvollzieher*: service in order; registered at the address until 1 July 2024. **Nutzer:** title at the window, mail in the building unusable — not verbatim in the corpus. |
| **Heuristik** | Anyone registered until a cut-off date, to whom the bailiff certifies proper service, has been served in law. |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | „Danach ist von einer ordnungsgemäßen Zustellung … auszugehen.“ **EN:** Thereafter, proper service is to be assumed. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | Judicial *assumption* of statutory *Zustellung* (“auszugehen”). Physical receipt is not the statutory measure and is not found as such in the reasons. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Carries § 750 ZPO in the *Erinnerung* as an assumption of *ordnungsgemäße Zustellung*, not as a finding that the title was in hand. |
| **Kontrolle** | Whether the window and unusable mail touch the statutory facts of *Zustellung* is not examined in the readable reasons. Missing receipt alone does not refute service. |

**June — new applications**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | Letter of 4 June: *Wiedereinsetzung*, § 144 ZPO, course packet, coordination. *Erinnerung* dismissed on 28 March. No *sofortige Beschwerde*. |
| **Heuristik** | Without *Beschwerde*, nothing is to be arranged on the letter. |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | Application for *Wiedereinsetzung* “bereits nicht nachvollziehbar”; § 766 ZPO again. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | Procedural *conclusion*, no functional finding. § 144 ZPO and the documents receive no status at all — they do not appear in the reply. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Closes the June applications in the reply, giving them no status. |
| **Kontrolle** | Whether the qualification lawfully made the June applications irrelevant is not discussed in the reply. A decision of its own on § 144 ZPO is not already due on that account. |

**Analyse:** The March shift lies in “auszugehen”: from registration and the bailiff’s file, proper *Zustellung* is taken as the load-bearing condition of § 750 ZPO. That is an assumption of statutory service. It is not refuted merely because physical receipt is missing or is not found in the reasons. What remains open is whether the objection was tested against the statutory concept of *Zustellung*. The June finding is not a status shift. It is **epistemic exclusion**: submitted applications and evidence without substantive consideration. They do not even receive the status of examined inadmissibility.

## 5. U — one-year limit and seeking support

**Court:** Landgericht Berlin II.  
**Decision:** judgment of 13 June 2025.

### 5.1 What the party applied for

**Gericht / Dok:** *Vollstreckungsbescheid* served on 24 August 2023. Letter of 18 February 2025, received at the *Mahngericht* on 21 February 2025, sought *Wiedereinsetzung*.

**Partei / Dok** (6 June 2025): *Wiedereinsetzung* because of a severe health crisis, with the course packet attached; application under § 144 ZPO; note that coordinated expert assessment was under consideration in a parallel case; stay until clarification; costs not to be imposed.

**Analyse:** The subject posed is unavoidable impediment plus taking of evidence. It is not “whether a year has passed”.

### 5.2 What the court made of it

**Gericht:** The letter of 18 February 2025 is to be construed as *Einspruch* plus *Wiedereinsetzung*. The *Einspruch* is inadmissible. The two-week time-limit expired on 7 September 2023. *Wiedereinsetzung* is no longer possible because more than one year has run from the end of the missed limit, § 234 Abs. 3 ZPO. The cut-off expired on 11 September 2024. The *Einspruch* was, as the defendant had been told, to be rejected without examining his objections and without a hearing. The submissions of 6 June 2025 change nothing.

The one-year period is a statutory cut-off and an **absolute Zeitgrenze**. It is compatible with the Basic Law (BVerfG, order of 18 December 1972). No exceptional case is made out. Such a case may arise if the default is attributable to the court’s sphere, or if the opponent lacks protectable reliance, for example in case of deceit. The default lies solely in the defendant’s sphere.

> Sofern der Beklagte aus gesundheitlichen Gründen über ein Jahr gehindert gewesen sein sollte, seine Rechte wahrzunehmen, hätte er sich Unterstützung suchen müssen.

**EN:** If the defendant should, for health reasons, have been prevented for more than a year from exercising his rights, he would have had to seek support.

### 5.3 Path of qualification

1. Application: unavoidable impediment, course packet, § 144 ZPO.  
2. Qualification: one-year limit of § 234 Abs. 3 ZPO.  
3. Exceptions narrowed to the court’s sphere and deceit.  
4. Health, if assumed, becomes a duty to “seek support”.  
5. § 144 ZPO is made pointless by the qualification; the letter of 6 June “changes nothing”. A decision of its own on the application is not already due on that account.  
6. Merits unexamined.

**Norm:** § 234 Abs. 3 ZPO is a statutory outer limit. Narrow exceptions do not turn it into a mere orientation.

**Analyse, alternative ground:** The one-year limit plus the court’s own list of exceptions can carry the tenor *if* health does not legally open an exception. Then the support-seeking sentence is not causal. That was the result of the previous draft.

**Recheck under § 2.5:** The year is independently calendar-based. It covers the *Einspruch* as such. If it lawfully made health — and thus the application under § 144 ZPO — irrelevant, the expert report needs no decision of its own. Precisely that allocation is the narrowing of the subject. It did not first arise from the support-seeking sentence; it arose from the exception list. The support-seeking sentence is an additional, hypothetical sentence in the reasons. BUF starts here with the qualification, not with a missing expert ruling.

**Level I, U:** A causal bypass *of the slogan* is not proved. The procedural question is not whether § 144 ZPO always required a decision of its own. It is whether the qualification (health outside the exceptions, therefore irrelevant) was wrong. If it was lawful, the expert report could fall away. That is not the claim that the one-year limit is soft.

**Level II, U:** The functional question was not examined. The legal consequence (no *Einspruch*, no merits) followed. The pattern “exit without establishing the original fact” is observed. On this row it is compatible with a self-standing outer time-limit.

### 5.4 Epistemic status shift

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | Service 24 August 2023; one-year limit expired on 11 September 2024 according to the reasons; letter of 6 June 2025 with course packet and application under § 144 ZPO. Exceptions named by the judgment itself: court’s sphere, deceit. |
| **Heuristik** | Anyone prevented by ill health for more than a year would have had to seek support. |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | The default lies in the party’s sphere. No exceptional case is made out. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | The support-seeking sentence is grammatically an *assumption* (“Sofern … gewesen sein sollte”). In the next step it is used as an *allocation to the party’s sphere*. That is the shift: subjunctive in the reasons, definite sphere for the exception. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Additional sentence. The tenor can stand on the year plus the exception list. The sentence nonetheless supplies the everyday bridge why health does not open the exception. |
| **Kontrolle** | Which support, whether it was possible: not examined. § 144 ZPO would be needed only if health remained material. The letter of 6 June “changes nothing”. |

**Analyse:** Here the epistemic finding is narrower than the causal one. The slogan need not carry the tenor. It still shifts a hypothesis into a finding of sphere. That shift remains even if the one-year limit alone carries the outcome.

## 6. A — the window and the day after

**Court:** Amtsgericht Kreuzberg.  
**Decision:** judgment of 22 July 2025.

### 6.1 What the party applied for

**Gericht / Dok:** *Vollstreckungsbescheid* served on 18 April 2024. Application on 3 April 2025. The *Mahngericht* asked on 8 April 2025 for a clear statement whether *Einspruch* was intended, and reminded on 7 May 2025. By letters of 9 and 16 May 2025 the defendant said he had filed *Einspruch* on 3 April and applied for *Wiedereinsetzung*.

**Dok** (filing 3 April 2025, full text checked, not in the BUF folder): *Wiedereinsetzung* under § 233 ZPO and an action against enforcement under § 767 ZPO. The default was without fault because of an exceptional health situation; evidence (*Verlaufspaket*) attached. No window January–July 2024 in this letter. No sentence that the obstacle had been removed.

**Dok** (letter 10 July 2025, full text checked, not in the BUF folder): Request for more time to file a medical statement. The statement was to explain restricted capacity to act in the period January to July 2024 and to found the *Wiedereinsetzungsantrag*. The statement had been requested, was not yet at court. No sentence that the obstacle had been removed after July 2024.

**Analyse:** The window January–July 2024 is in the letter of 10 July, not in that of 3 April. *Eine Anerkennung des Hindernisendes ist in den geprüften Parteischriften nicht enthalten.*

### 6.2 What the court made of it

**Gericht:** *Einspruch* inadmissible. Time-limit expired on 2 May 2024. *Wiedereinsetzung* under § 233 ZPO not to be granted, because the application was not made within two weeks of removal of the obstacle (§ 234 Abs. 1, 2 ZPO).

> Wenn man zu seinen Gunsten als wahr unterstellt, dass er — wie er es vorträgt — krankheitsbedingt in der Zeit von Januar 2024 bis Juli 2024 eingeschränkt handlungsfähig war, so ist nicht ersichtlich, warum er dann nicht bis Mitte August 2024 Einspruch hätte einlegen können.

**EN:** If one assumes in his favour as true that he — as he pleads — was of restricted capacity to act by reason of illness from January 2024 to July 2024, it is not apparent why he could not then have filed an objection (*Einspruch*) by mid-August 2024.

The extension for documents is not to be granted.

> Auf diese Unterlagen kommt es aus den genannten Gründen nicht an.

**EN:** Those documents are, for the reasons stated, of no consequence.

### 6.3 Path of qualification

1. Application 3 April: *Wiedereinsetzung* for impediment, no window. 10 July: window January–July 2024 and time for a statement.  
2. Qualification: two-week limit from removal of the obstacle.  
3. Equation: end of the pleaded window = obstacle removed.  
4. Calendar step: mid-August.  
5. Documents for exactly that window: irrelevant.  
6. *Einspruch* rejected.

**Analyse:** A described window of impairment is not a declaration that the obstacle was then gone. *Eine Anerkennung des Hindernisendes ist in den geprüften Parteischriften nicht enthalten.*

Separate from that remains the *Darlegung* question: whether the duration of the impediment and the moment of its removal were adequately set out. That is not the same operation as the calendar step to mid-August.

The sentence “nicht ersichtlich, warum nicht” is the additional **gerichtliche Tatsachenannahme** between pleading and legal consequence. Without it, § 234 Abs. 2 ZPO has no starting day, insofar as removal is not otherwise established.

**Level I, A:** The step to mid-August is **gerichtliche Tatsachenannahme** (assumed window → treated *Hindernisende*), load-bearing for the two-week limit, and can be **Sachkundeersatz**. It is not marked as an **Entscheidung wegen unzureichender Darlegung**: the court does not say that removal or duration was not set out, and refuse on that ground; it takes the window as true and sets the day after as the start of the limit.

Irrelevance of the documents follows from this calendar step. That is points 3 and 6 of the qualification path in one movement.

Strength of the evidence that the court moved from a named period of impairment to an unestablished date of removal: **8.5 / 10**, if the originals confirm the content given here. A is then one of the most convincing examples in BUF, because not only the court’s reasons but the party’s original filings can be compared.

### 6.4 Epistemic status shift

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | **Dok** 3 April: exceptional situation, no window, no removal. **Dok** 10 July: impairment January–July 2024; time for a statement not yet at court; no acknowledgement of *Hindernisende*. |
| **Heuristik** | After the end of a named window, the next half of the calendar is available again. “Nicht ersichtlich, warum nicht.” |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | *Einspruch* could have been filed by mid-August 2024. The two-week limit of § 234 Abs. 2 ZPO was missed. The documents do not matter. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | The window is expressly introduced as an *assumption* (“als wahr unterstellt”). The *Hindernisende* is not marked as an assumption. It follows as everyday certainty. Shift: assumed impairment → treated end of the obstacle. That is *Tatsachenannahme*, not a finding of insufficient *Darlegung*. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Load-bearing for refusal of *Wiedereinsetzung*. Irrelevance of the documents hangs on this shift. |
| **Kontrolle** | Acknowledgement of the end in the party filings: not contained. Whether duration and moment of removal were adequately set out: a separate question, not marked in the reasons as the ground of refusal. |

**Analyse:** This is the sharpest status shift of the five rows. The court names the assumption and, in the next sentence, lets it stand as the start of the time-limit. The party filings do not support that step.

## 7. KB — connecting facts and the circle

**Court:** Amtsgericht Kreuzberg.  
**Decision:** judgment of 10 July 2025.

### 7.1 What the party applied for

**Gericht / Dok:** *Vollstreckungsbescheid* served on 4 July 2024. On 4 April 2025 *Einspruch* with medical documents referred to, and an application for *Wiedereinsetzung* because of health-related impairment. In the reasons: application for an expert report under § 144 ZPO; court request for further documents.

**Analyse:** The subject was unavoidable impediment in the window of the *Einspruch* limit (until 28 July 2024) plus the statutory means of clarification.

### 7.2 What the court made of it

**Gericht:** The statutory conditions of *Wiedereinsetzung* were not set out. The certificate of inpatient stays records stays only until 3 January 2023. The same for a course letter of 18 July 2023 and a certificate of attendance of 4 April 2024. Further documents that could show the health-related absence of fault as to the time-limit were not filed despite a court request.

> Die vom Beklagten beantragte Begutachtung nach § 144 ZPO konnte daher mangels Anknüpfungstatsachen nicht erfolgen.

**EN:** The expert examination applied for by the defendant under § 144 ZPO could therefore not take place for lack of connecting facts (*Anknüpfungstatsachen*).

Without an admissible *Einspruch*, no examination of the claim on the merits.

### 7.3 Path of qualification

1. Application: impediment in the July 2024 window, documents, expert report.  
2. Qualification: setting out unavoidable impediment *in this window*.  
3. Time reading: pieces before the window = insufficient.  
4. *Anknüpfung* denied.  
5. Expert report refused.  
6. Not set out → *Einspruch* inadmissible.

Four quantities that do not coincide: *Anknüpfungstatsachen*, *Darlegung*, facts still to be proved, the expert question. The circle arises when 3 and 4 are made a condition of 1.

**Recheck of the alternative “no Anknüpfung”:** The calendar of the pieces against July 2024 speaks for a time gap. Comparing dates is not special expertise. A course of illness can still be *Anknüpfung* without falling inside the window. The alternative carries if *Anknüpfung* and the main fact stay separate *and* *Anknüpfung* is nonetheless missing. It does not carry automatically merely because the dates are earlier.

**Level I, KB:** Primarily a **burden-of-proof decision**. The tenor can stand on “not set out” without the expert sentence. The expert sentence is causal for closing the route to an expert report. § 139 ZPO: there was a request for further documents (**Gericht**). That does not exclude that the burden was so laid that the requested means already presupposed its own order.

### 7.4 Epistemic status shift

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | Documents dated to January 2023, July 2023, April 2024. Material according to the reasons: *Einspruch* limit end of July 2024. Further pieces not filed despite request. Application under § 144 ZPO. |
| **Heuristik** | What is dated before the “hier interessierenden Zeitraum” (the period at issue) supplies no *Anknüpfung* for an expert report on that period. |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | Conditions of *Wiedereinsetzung* not set out. Expert report “mangels Anknüpfungstatsachen” not possible. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | The dates of the pieces are *established facts* (calendar). The sentence that *Anknüpfungstatsachen* are missing is a *conclusion* about what a course can carry. Shift: date → absence of an expert basis, presented as a fact of the proceeding. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Closes the route to an expert report. The tenor can additionally stand on “not set out”. |
| **Kontrolle** | The counter-assumption — a course can be *Anknüpfung* without falling inside the window — is not discussed. |

**Analyse:** The calendar is ordinary evaluation. The status shift lies in “mangels Anknüpfungstatsachen konnte daher … nicht erfolgen”: a temporal reading is treated as the exhausted basis of expertise.

## 8. TB\* — transfer test, different subject-matter

**Court:** Amtsgericht Lichtenberg.  
**Decision:** judgment after the hearing of 11 August 2026, pronounced on 25 August 2026.  
**Not an annex case of the original BUF.**

### 8.1 What the party applied for

**Partei / Dok** (application at the hearing, 11 August 2026): admission of annex BUF to the file. No new medical finding. No application for an expert. The question is the direction of the inference:

> Tatsachen → Ergebnis  
> oder  
> Ergebnis → Tatsachenannahme?

**EN:** Facts → result, or result → assumption of facts?

> Die AU-Tage sind feststellbar. Der daraus abgeleitete Gesundheitszustand ist es nicht automatisch.

**EN:** The days of incapacity for work (AU) can be established. The state of health inferred from them cannot automatically.

In short: What is found? On what factual basis? By what method or expertise? How is the result reviewable?

The dispute itself is another: membership, use, extraordinary termination, contributions — not *Wiedereinsetzung* against a *Vollstreckungsbescheid*.

### 8.2 What the court made of it

**Gericht:** Claim unfounded, counterclaim well founded. Conclusion of the contract undisputed. Withdrawal not in time. Notice of 10 May 2023 does not end the first term early, but only on 31 March 2024. No warning under § 314 Abs. 2 BGB pleaded. Change of residence does not carry: move into the flat stated in the contract on 1 March 2023, before the contract.

> Der Verweis auf eine Arbeitsunfähigkeit an einzelnen Tagen in den Monaten März und April 2023 genügt nicht, eine außerordentliche Vertragsbeendigung zu begründen […] Im Übrigen lassen die Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigungen gerade nicht erkennen, dass der Kläger nicht in der Lage gewesen ist, die Angebote der Beklagten zu nutzen. Das eigene Handeln des Klägers widerspricht dem, denn er hat auch nach seinen eigenen Angaben Termine für das Sportstudio der Beklagten gebucht.

**EN:** The reference to incapacity for work on individual days in March and April 2023 is not sufficient to establish an extraordinary termination of the contract […] Moreover, the certificates of incapacity for work do not show that the claimant was unable to use the defendant’s facilities. The claimant’s own conduct contradicts that, because even on his own account he booked appointments at the defendant’s sports studio.

The notice of termination said only that the claimant was unable to pay the invoices. Whether later grounds could still be relied on under § 242 BGB could be left open. Appeal not admitted: the warning is settled law; whether a move or an illness is good cause is a matter of the individual case.

BUF is not mentioned in the reasons.

### 8.3 Path of qualification

1. Party: opening of use / counter-performance / health-related inability to use; before judgment, the method of knowledge.  
2. Court: contract, withdrawal, warning, risk of changed personal circumstances, AU days, booking.  
3. Health-related inability to use is not treated as an established quantity, but as not recognisable and contradicted by booking.  
4. The method question posed at the hearing remains unanswered in the reasons.

### 8.4 Recheck of the alternatives

| Alternative | Self-standing? | Covers all applications? | Depends on unestablished function? | Arose from narrowing? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No timely withdrawal | yes, from the contract copy | not the health pleading | no | no |
| No warning for breach of duty | yes, *if* the good cause is a breach by the other side | not necessarily the health ground (which need not be a breach by the other side) | no | partly: the breach track narrows good cause |
| Risk of changed personal circumstances | legal rule, not a fact | can cut off the health ground in law | no — it makes the function irrelevant | yes: qualification “personal risk”, not a functional finding |
| Notice text only inability to pay | expressly left open | no | — | — |
| AU days / booking | no, additional sentence | the health pleading | yes | yes |

**Analyse:** Withdrawal and, on the breach track, the warning can carry parts of the tenor. The health pleading is not disposed of by withdrawal. It is disposed of by the rule on personal risk *or* by AU/booking. The first variant is qualification of the subject. The second is the BUF sentence. The previous draft treated the tenor as a whole as viable without the booking inference. That remains tenable for the payment order. It does not refute that the functional assumption, *insofar as* it cuts off the health ground, is not set out.

The booking is in the judgment a **counter-indicator**, not final proof of full capacity to use. An organisational act and use of the facilities are different quantities.

**Level I, TB\*:** Causal bypass of the slogan for the whole tenor: not proved. Unanswered method question of the BUF application: observed. Whether *Gehör* on application points 3–4 was breached is a separate procedural question; it does not follow merely from BUF not being cited in the reasons.

**Level II, TB\*:** Transfer positive as observation: even with a different subject-matter, a sentence about capacity arises from an auxiliary act without the bridge the application demanded. Transfer negative as proof: TB\* does not prove the four original cases.

### 8.5 Epistemic status shift

| | |
|---|---|
| **Ausgangsinformation** | AU on individual days in March/April 2023, first certificates; AU also before the contract (7 March 2023); appointment bookings on the party’s own account. BUF application: AU days ≠ automatically a state of health; set out the bridge. |
| **Heuristik** | Whoever books appointments thereby contradicts an inability to use the facilities. AU days do not prove inability to use. |
| **Schlussfolgerung** | Extraordinary termination for illness not made out. |
| **Erkenntnisstatus** | “Lassen … nicht erkennen” is a *negative finding* on the certificates. “Widerspricht” treats the booking as a *counter-indicator*, yet in syntax as a contradiction of inability — closer to a *factual consequence* than to a bounded indicator. Shift: organisational act → functional counter-proof. |
| **Entscheidungsfunktion** | Cuts off the health ground. The payment order has other supports (withdrawal, warning, risk rule). |
| **Kontrolle** | The BUF application had asked exactly for the limit of this inference. The reasons do not draw it: booking ≠ use is not discussed. |

**Analyse:** Transfer of the status shift: an indicator becomes a contradiction in the wording. The court need not find full capacity to use. It must mark the limit when it deploys the indicator against the pleaded inability. It does not mark it.

## 9. Comparison — no artificial, no hidden incompatibility

The same person. Essentially the same course packet in U, A, NP (June) and KB. TB\* uses AU days and bookings; that is different material.

The five slogans are not five diagnoses.

| Case | Original subject (party) | Court’s qualification | Unresolved fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NP** | Service, performance, exemption from attachment; later impediment + § 144 | § 766; nichts veranlasst | Objection to proper *Zustellung* examined? Qualification of the June applications as irrelevant correct? |
| **U** | Impediment + § 144 | One-year limit + exception list | Ability to seek help, if that is to close the exception |
| **A** | Impediment; window in the letter of 10 July | Two weeks from assumed removal | *Hindernisende* not acknowledged; whether duration/removal was set out — separate question |
| **KB** | Impediment in the July 2024 window + § 144 | *Darlegung* / *Anknüpfung* | Whether the course can be *Anknüpfung* for July 2024 |
| **TB\*** | Use / counter-performance / method of functional evaluation | Contract, warning, risk, counter-indicator | Capacity to use; bridge AU → function |

**Analyse:** These are different procedural rules in different situations. No logical incompatibility of health courses follows.

It also does not follow that there is no real incompatibility. Incompatible in the functional sense would be: treating the same material act in the same period once as possible and once as impossible. These sentences do not necessarily do that, because they concern different acts (seeking help, filing *Einspruch*, meeting a time-limit in July 2024, using a studio).

What they *can* do in common: not establish the original fact and let the legal consequence follow nonetheless. That is Level II. It is not five times the same violation of law.

The epistemic findings in the reasons — *Statuswechsel* or *Ausschluss*:

| Case | Status on the file | Status in the reasons | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NP March** | Objection + bailiff’s note + registration | “auszugehen” of service | Statuswechsel (assumption of statutory *Zustellung* carries § 750) |
| **NP June** | Applications and packet on the file | no status; “nichts veranlasst” | **Ausschluss** (without substantive consideration) |
| **U** | Subjunctive “sofern … sollte” | party’s sphere | Statuswechsel (assumption → finding of sphere) |
| **A** | “als wahr unterstellt” (window) | mid-August as start of the limit | Statuswechsel (assumption → end of obstacle) |
| **KB** | Dates of the pieces | “mangels Anknüpfungstatsachen” | Statuswechsel (calendar → exhausted expert basis) |
| **TB\*** | Booking as an act | “widerspricht” inability | Statuswechsel (indicator → contradiction) |

**Analyse:** Not every heuristic is the same shift. A shifts most clearly from a marked assumption to an unmarked end. U still marks the subjunctive and yet uses it as a sphere. TB\* stays closer to an indicator but does not draw the limit. NP June is not a status shift: the applications receive no status. That is epistemic exclusion — without substantive consideration. Level II describes different epistemic types — not five diagnoses.

### Level I

| **TB\*** | Method application unanswered; booking as counter-indicator | payment order has other supports | slogan not solely causal |

### Level II

What is observed is a recurring treatment of functional uncertainty: the original question is re-qualified, narrowed, declared irrelevant, placed behind a bar, or skipped with an auxiliary fact. The statutes differ. The function can be the same — leaving the central fact unresolved.

That is the result of different lawful devices *or* of a recurring definition of the subject of inquiry. The two must be kept apart. Motives are not proved. Coordination of judges is not proved. The offered cross-proceeding expert report is proved as a party application, without a ruling of its own in Lichtenberg (**Dok** 4 and 7 June 2025), and declared irrelevant at the Landgericht by qualification (**Gericht** 13 June 2025: the letter of 6 June changes nothing). Whether an expert report was due depends on the qualification, not on the missing expert ruling as such.

## 12. Extended conception of BUF

The historical formula remains: **BUF = U + A + NP + KB**, later observed **+ TB\***. The slogans are names of empirical cases. They are not renamed.

The analytical model must go beyond *Tatsachensubstitution*.

| Mechanism | Case in which it primarily appears |
|---|---|
| **Narrowing of the subject** | U (year instead of impediment); TB\* (risk instead of capacity to use); NP June (no *Beschwerde* instead of new applications) |
| **Irrelevance of evidence** | A (documents); KB (pieces before the window) |
| **Procedural bar** | NP (§ 766; nichts veranlasst) |
| **Burden of proof / circle** | KB |
| **Normative expectation** | U (Unterstützung suchen) — in the reasons, not necessarily in the tenor |
| **Auxiliary fact / counter-indicator** | TB\* (booking) |
| **Calendar step as end of obstacle** | A |
| **Epistemic status shift** (*Statuswechsel*) | A (sharpest); U; KB; TB\*; NP March only as assumption of statutory *Zustellung* — not all five |
| **Epistemic exclusion** (*Ausschluss*) | NP June: submitted applications and evidence without substantive consideration (“nichts veranlasst”) |

“Bypass” names the *function*: a fact necessary for the legal consequence is not established on the path the statute provides. The legal type is to be named case by case. Not every type is substitution of a medical fact.

§ 144 ZPO remains the statutory means where special expertise is material to the decision. Its non-application is not automatically unlawful. If the court has lawfully treated the fact as irrelevant, the expert report may fall away; a decision of its own on the application is then not due. Non-application needs explanation if the court answers a technically demanding question that the legal consequence requires — or if the qualification of irrelevance is wrong. BUF starts with that qualification, not with the missing expert ruling as such.

## 13. The document packet — what it carries, what it does not

**Dok / Analyse:** In U, A, NP (June) and KB the civil courts had a course packet: contacts, stays, a longer course. It sets no end-date of impairment. It certifies no civil-law capacity to meet a time-limit. It does not contain the court sentences “Unterstützung suchen”, “Mitte August”, “nicht zu prüfen”, “keine Anknüpfungstatsachen”.

**EN:** “seek support”, “mid-August”, “not to be examined”, “no connecting facts” (*Anknüpfungstatsachen*).

Documented treatment is not the same as missing capacity to meet a time-limit. The absence of an express proof that capacity was missing is not positive proof that capacity existed. A booking of an appointment is first a booking of an appointment.

## 14. Procedural responsibility

**Norm:** Art. 97 Abs. 1 GG protects against extraneous influence, not against being bound by law. Art. 103 Abs. 1 GG, § 139 ZPO, § 286 ZPO remain. They neither abolish the party’s burden of pleading nor create investigation of the court’s own motion in civil procedure. Inadequate party pleading and omitted judicial *Hinweis* are to be examined separately.

A case question, not a slogan:

> Hat das Gericht die gesetzlichen Mittel der Klärung benutzt, bevor es die Unzulänglichkeit des Vortrags zur Grundlage der negativen Entscheidung machte?

**EN:** Did the court use the statutory means of clarification before it made the inadequacy of the pleading the basis of the negative decision?

**Analyse:** The means of clarification are not due where the fact is lawfully irrelevant. Then the qualification is to be tested, not the absence of an expert report.

## 15. Consequence on the merits

Where the preliminary question bars the merits, titled sums and discrepant records remain behind admissibility. That is the consequence of a closed preliminary question — whether or not the closing was a proved bypass.

For three chargeable original decisions the *Justizkasse* billed €831 in total. A fourth decision was free of court fees. Court-appointed experts in the four original decisions: **none**. A counting finding, not an automatically established breach of § 144 ZPO.

## 16. How to read the formula

The strongest BUF does not claim that judges circumvented the statute five times. It names a criterion, reconstructs the application first, tests the epistemic status of each inference, and says where an assumption is then used as fact.

Five different procedural answers do not mean five different errors of law. They can show a repeated leaving of the central question unresolved.

| | What remains | What is no longer claimed |
|---|---|---|
| **U** | Outer time-limit; qualification of the subject; support-seeking sentence not causal | The limit is a mere orientation; fivefold circumvention |
| **A** | Window ≠ removal of obstacle; documents irrelevant; no acknowledgement of the end in the filings checked | The party acknowledged the end; refusal was only insufficient *Darlegung* |
| **NP** | Original subject service/performance; June: nichts veranlasst; no functional finding | Coordination creates a duty to join; “nichts zu bestätigen” as wording |
| **KB** | Burden of proof; possible circle; time gap of the pieces | Documents are automatically *Anknüpfung*; the court found the person “able” |
| **TB\*** | Transfer test; booking as counter-indicator; withdrawal/warning do not fully cover the health ground | BUF is causal in this case for the whole tenor |
